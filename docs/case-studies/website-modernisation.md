# Case study: Large-scale website modernisation

> A four-month rebuild of a public website, migrating more than 1,000 pages from Vue.js and Prismic to Next.js and Hygraph.

The organisation and client are intentionally not named here. The case study focuses on the engineering work and delivery scope.

## The challenge

The project was a modernisation of a large public website. The existing Vue.js and Prismic setup made it difficult to sustain a shared content model and reusable page structures as the site evolved.

The work was broader than a framework migration: the team also needed to deliver a rebrand, a redesigned design system and a content model that would let marketing manage pages without requiring engineering changes for every page.

## My role

I was the technical lead and sole technical owner for the rebuild. I led the technical direction and worked with a five-person cross-functional team to deliver the migration, rebrand and content model within four months.

My remit covered the frontend platform change and the technical implementation of the new page and content structures.

## Key engineering decisions

### Move to Next.js

We replaced the Vue.js frontend with Next.js as the foundation for the rebuilt public website.

### Move content to Hygraph

We replaced Prismic with Hygraph and introduced a shared content model to support flexible page structures and marketing-managed content.

### Use Incremental Static Regeneration

With more than 1,000 pages in scope, the implementation used Next.js Incremental Static Regeneration (ISR) for page delivery.

### Build reusable page structures and a design system

The migration moved pages onto reusable components and flexible page structures. This work was delivered alongside the rebrand and redesigned design system, rather than treating the migration as a like-for-like framework swap.

## Delivery

A five-person cross-functional team delivered the platform migration, rebrand and content model in four months. More than 1,000 pages were moved to the new platform.

The resulting content model allows the marketing team to publish and manage pages without needing engineering to build each page individually.

## Outcome

- More than 1,000 pages migrated from Vue.js and Prismic to Next.js and Hygraph.
- Reusable components and flexible page structures established as part of the rebuild.
- A redesigned design system delivered alongside the rebrand.
- Marketing can publish through the content model without engineering implementation for every page.
- Delivery completed by a five-person cross-functional team in four months.

- Balancing a platform rebuild with rebrand and design-system delivery.
